About me

Operations-first product thinking.

I'm a Bachelor in Nutrition and Culinary Specialist turned Product Manager. For over 15 years, I worked hands-on in the food industry, leading operations, kitchens, and quality systems. CookUnity 🔍 Research

Designing a user-centered advanced filtering system

Redesigning CookUnity's advanced filtering system around users' mental models as part of the Core UX initiative.

Core UX Implementation
Challenge
Users struggled to find meals that matched their preferences because the filtering experience was confusing, inconsistent, and built around our internal taxonomy rather than users' mental models. The goal was to redesign the entire filtering system as part of CookUnity's Core UX initiative to reduce friction and improve meal discovery.
My role
Owned the Advanced Filters initiative, partnering with Product, UX Research, Design, Copy, and Engineering. Led the research synthesis and translated user insights into a new information architecture, filter taxonomy, naming strategy, nutritional logic, and product requirements.
Result
  • Redesigned the entire advanced filtering system based on UX research and tree testing
  • Reorganized filters around users' mental models instead of internal categorization
  • Defined new taxonomy, copy, tagging logic, and nutritional criteria across diets, proteins, cuisines, and ingredient exclusions
  • Established the filtering framework adopted within the Core UX redesign
